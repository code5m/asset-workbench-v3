# Product Information Architecture

The app has four top-level entries:

1. Introduction
2. Guide
3. Asset Space
4. Workbench

## Introduction

Explains the product in human language.

## Guide

Walks through first import.

## Asset Space

Allows users to enter directories and files across code, design, docs,
conversations, and derived outputs.

## Workbench

Shows technical version state and import pipeline details.
